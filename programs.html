DARTT Lab

Coordinated the Truist Data Analytics, Research & Technology Lab — a $350,000 gift from Truist Bank to the College of Business. Ran SPSS and Python trainings, the National HBCU Stock Market Challenge, and a Georgetown University environmental-data collaboration. For the 2023 Stock Market Challenge alone: outreach to 350+ students, 42 at the info session, and 18 mentorship hours logged.
