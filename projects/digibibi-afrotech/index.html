DigiBibi at AfroTech 2025

A digital storytelling brand that represented Bowie State University's Center for Data Analytics at AfroTech 2025 in Houston.

Overview

What it was

DigiBibi was created to show the data talent and cultural innovation coming out of HBCUs. As Program Manager for the Center for Data Analytics, I developed the brand, coordinated the presence at the conference, and handled engagement with industry attendees.
