Youth program · High school

Sports Analytics Camp

Introducing high school students to data analytics through the sports they already follow, in partnership with the Brian Westbrook Foundation.

Overview

What it was

The camp used sports as a way into data. Eighteen high school students worked with real sports statistics, from player performance to game strategy, and learned how data collection, analysis, and visualization apply to questions they already care about.

My role

Curriculum designer and facilitator

Curriculum

Program design

Designed the curriculum covering data collection, analysis, and visualization for high school students.

Teaching

Facilitation

Facilitated hands-on sessions and taught Excel and basic data visualization.

Partnership

Brian Westbrook Foundation

Coordinated the partnership with the foundation.

Method

How it worked

Datasets

Real sports data

Activities used NFL, NBA, and soccer statistics that students could connect to directly.

Projects

Student analyses

Student teams analyzed player performance and game strategy, then presented their findings.

Careers

Pathways into data

Sessions connected data skills to careers in sports, business, and technology.
