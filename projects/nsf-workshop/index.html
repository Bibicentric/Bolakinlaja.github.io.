National workshop · NSF Discovery

Data and the African Diaspora

A national NSF Discovery Workshop on culturally relevant data science education for HBCU educators.

Overview

What it was

The workshop brought HBCU educators together to build data science curricula that draw on African Diaspora histories, perspectives, and datasets, so that students see their own communities in the analytics they learn.
