Student consulting · Entrepreneurship

EIC Market Research Collaboration

Student-led market research for entrepreneurs, run by the Center for Data Analytics with the Entrepreneurship Innovation Center.

Overview

What it is

The collaboration gives data students real consulting experience while giving campus entrepreneurs professional market research they could not otherwise afford. Students are matched with entrepreneur clients based on industry and need, then produce research and recommendations for each client.

My role

Program design and management

Design

Program structure

Designed and managed the collaboration, including the partnership between the Center and the Entrepreneurship Innovation Center.

Students

Recruiting and training

Recruited and trained student researchers in professional research methods.

Clients

Matching and scoping

Matched student teams with entrepreneur clients and facilitated client intake and project scoping.

Quality

Review and mentorship

Provided quality review and mentorship throughout each research project.

Next

Where it goes from here

The plan is to formalize the consulting training curriculum, build external partnerships with local businesses, and explore grant funding for student researcher stipends.
