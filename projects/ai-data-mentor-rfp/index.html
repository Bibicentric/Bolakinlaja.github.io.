Johns Hopkins University · AI Business Strategy

AI Data Mentor Platform — District RFP Response

A capstone RFP response proposing a compliant, teacher-governed AI platform for high school data analytics instruction.

Overview

What it is

A course assignment for Johns Hopkins' AI Business Strategy certificate program (February 2026): respond to a school district's RFP for an AI tool to support high school Data Analytics instruction.

I proposed a secure, teacher-governed AI Data Mentor platform: career-simulated learning pathways across Business Intelligence, Public Health Analytics, and Sports Data Science, integrated with the district's existing systems (Google Workspace for Education, Infinite Campus, ClassLink SSO), and built so teachers retain override authority over every AI-generated suggestion.

My approach

Comply, govern, finance, deploy

Comply

Regulatory design

COPPA and FERPA-aligned data governance, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility, role-based access control, and encryption at rest and in transit, built in rather than bolted on.

Govern

Human-in-the-loop

Teacher override on every AI suggestion, quarterly bias and fairness audits, model drift monitoring, and transparent KPI reporting to administrators.

Finance

Full cost model

Five-year total cost of ownership ($880K), cost-per-student breakdown, and a 24-month breakeven projection built on documented, stated assumptions.

Deploy

Phased rollout

A four-phase Recognize–Organize–Analyze–Deploy sequence with tiered, dependency-ordered implementation to reduce operational risk before scaling district-wide.

Note

What this is, and isn't

This was a graded capstone responding to a hypothetical district RFP scenario, not a live engagement or a real product. I'm presenting it because it shows the financial and governance rigor behind a strategy proposal, not just the strategic framing.
