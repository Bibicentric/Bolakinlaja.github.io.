Future Tech Hackathon: 1st and 2nd Place

Prince George's County · Capitol Technology University · September 18, 2026

Fourteen Bowie State students built three AI-powered civic applications and took both 1st and 2nd place overall:

  • REACH (1st place) — a real-time emergency-response and incident-intelligence platform for Prince George's County, built to help people get assistance faster when every second matters, for a county of nearly 970,000 residents and 157,000+ households across 45 fire and EMS stations, with 400+ emergency responses a day.
  • MamaCare PG (2nd place) — an AI-powered platform helping pregnant women and new mothers quickly find maternal-health and community resources tailored to their needs.
  • PGC Smart Assist — an AI-powered civic intelligence platform using real-time community and public-safety data to help residents make safer, more informed decisions.

Sponsors and partners included Prince George's County Government, Amazon Web Services, Capitol Technology University, and Accenture. The county's Office of Information Technology also presented me with its Community Solutions Award for organizing CDA's participation.

What matters most to me is not the awards. It is watching students apply their skills to real problems, adapt under pressure, and present their ideas to industry leaders with confidence.

Summer 2026 Research Cohort

Center for Data Analytics · with Dr. Eona Harrison · July 7 – August 23, 2026

Students tackled real-world challenges in healthcare, transportation, housing, responsible gambling and AI, and presented their insights to leaders across sectors. Partners included Prince George's County government and the U.S. Census Bureau.

I co-lead the Community Data & Civic Analytics Lab, built around the Prince George's County Open Data Portal, Data.gov and Tableau Public.
