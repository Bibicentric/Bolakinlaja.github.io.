NexaFlow Analytics

An AI-powered market intelligence platform. Entrepreneurs and small business owners answer a few questions, pay once, and get a market analysis in minutes — built on real government data (Census, BLS, IRS, FRED), not a chatbot guess. The pitch, in plain terms: the same category of research a consultant charges $1,000–$10,000+ for, or a report license from IBISWorld costs $800–$2,850, delivered for $29–$99, specific to your exact business, with no subscription.

Data Wayfinder™

A free public data discovery tool. It helps students, educators, researchers, and community members find credible public data faster — verified demographic, economic, education, and health indicators across all 50 U.S. states and 168 countries, with one-click links straight to the official federal and international data portals behind each number.

No account required, nothing to sign up for. Built for classrooms and labs first: it runs as a small local server so an instructor can put it in front of a room of students on one network, with automated weekly checks that its links to the underlying government portals still work.
