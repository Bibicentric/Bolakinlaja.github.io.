NexaFlow Analytics

An AI-powered market intelligence platform. Entrepreneurs and small business owners submit their business idea, pay, and get a structured market analysis in under 10 minutes for under $100 — built on real U.S. government data (Census, BLS, IRS, FRED), the same category of research consultants charge thousands of dollars for.

Built end to end: product strategy, system architecture, the Supabase data layer, Stripe payments, and the GPT-4o report generation pipeline, on Next.js and deployed on Vercel. Pricing is tiered for three real audiences — pre-launch founders validating an idea, early-stage founders deciding whether to double down, and investor-ready founders who need citable, professional-grade data for a pitch deck or loan application.

In development

Data Wayfinder™

A free public data discovery tool. It helps students, educators, researchers, and community members find credible public data faster — verified demographic, economic, education, and health indicators across all 50 U.S. states and 168 countries, with one-click links straight to the official federal and international data portals behind each number.

No account required, nothing to sign up for. Built for classrooms and labs first: it runs as a small local server so an instructor can put it in front of a room of students on one network, with automated weekly checks that its links to the underlying government portals still work.
